# The Group Ironman Homebase

Made for Group Ironman groups, so you can stop asking each other who has what. It puts the whole group in one sidebar panel: what everyone's carrying and banking, who took what out of group storage and how close you all are to your goals. It'll also settle who has the fastest Brutus. There's no website or account to sign up for, just the plugin.

## Features

| Where | Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Members tab | Member cards | Everyone in your group, online or not, with their world, combat level, total level and when you last heard from them. |
| Members tab | Live bars | HP, prayer, run energy and spec for anyone who's online. |
| Members tab | Skills | Every skill, laid out like the in-game skills tab. |
| Members tab | Quests | Quests and miniquests done, quest points and the full quest list. |
| Members tab | Achievement diaries | Which tiers they've finished, region by region. |
| Members tab | Combat achievements | Points and tier. |
| Members tab | Collection log | Slots filled, and each page they've shared. |
| Members tab | Kill counts | Every boss they've killed, with their PB. Bosses from before they installed it come from the hiscores. |
| Members tab | Items | Inventory, equipment, bank, seed vault and looting bag. |
| Members tab | House | Useful furniture they've built, and what's in their costume room. |
| Search box | Item search | Who has an item, how many and where. Group storage counts too. |
| Storage tab | Group storage | What's in it, and who last looked. |
| Storage tab | History | Who put in or took out what, and when. |
| Storage tab | Owed back | Mark the items that should come back. Anyone who takes one out stays on the list until they return it. |
| Storage tab | Bounties and held items | What's locked for a goal, and what's kept for someone's request. |
| Updates tab | Catch-up | Levels, quests, diaries, combat achievements, collection log slots, kill counts, PBs, good drops, pets and deaths, each with a picture. |
| Updates tab | Your own updates | Your own can show up there too, if you turn it on. |
| Requests tab | Item requests | Ask the group for an item. Whoever takes it on trades it to you or leaves it in group storage, where it's kept for you. |
| Goals tab | Goals | Targets for everyone or for one of you: a skill level, total level, quest, diary, kill count, combat achievement points or collection log slots. Everyone's progress shows as a bar. |
| Goals tab | Bounties | Lock some of an item in group storage until someone reaches a goal. |
| Compare tab | Compare | Any two of you side by side, from levels and quest points to kill counts, PBs and hiscore ranks. It tells you who's winning. |
| In game | Group HUD | Everyone's bars, combat level and location in the corner of the screen, Sailing islands included. Pick what shows, its size and order, compact mode, and whether it only lists your world. |
| In game | Find on map | Click or right-click someone in the HUD, then open your world map to see where they are. |
| In game | World map | Your group shows as named arrows. Anyone off screen sits at the edge, pointing the way. |
| In game | Map pins | Right-click the world map to pin a spot for the group. |
| In game | Pings | Middle-click the ground or a monster to flash it for everyone, with a sound and a dot on the minimap. You can use a key and left-click instead. |
| In game | Group highlights | Outline a monster for the group. It can stay outlined when it respawns. |
| In game | Group tiles | Mark tiles everyone in the group sees, even in raids. |
| In game | Collection log | Dots in each groupmate's colour on the items they have, and their kill counts and PBs next to yours on boss pages. |
| In game | Chat messages | A chat line when someone in the group levels up, asks for something, reaches a goal or drops a pin. Each kind can be turned off. |
| Settings | Your colour | One colour for your arrow, pin, pings, highlights, tiles and name. |
| Settings | What you share | Stop sharing your position, inventory, equipment, bank, seed vault, looting bag, drops and pets, or deaths. |
| Behind the scenes | Catching up | Everyone keeps a copy of the group's data, and whoever's online passes it along, so you catch up on someone you're never online with. |
| Behind the scenes | Member checks | Everyone in the party is checked against your group's member list. If someone else gets in, nothing but your name is shared until they leave. |

## Getting started

Log in on your Group Ironman account, open the house icon in the sidebar and press **Connect**. The plugin finds your group by itself. Everyone in the group needs it, and you press Connect each time you start RuneLite.

Not a GIM group? One of you presses **Create group** and sends round the passphrase. The others paste it into the plugin's settings.

## Controls

- Middle-click the ground or a monster to ping it.
- Shift + right-click a monster for **Group highlight**, or a tile for **Group mark**.
- Right-click the world map for **Pin for group**.
- To find someone, click them in the HUD and then open your world map.
- Right-click an item on the Storage tab to say it needs returning.

## Good to know

- Data only moves while two of you are online at the same time.
- Banks and group storage update when someone opens them.
- PBs come from RuneLite's Chat Commands plugin, which is on by default.
- Bounties and items held for requests only stop withdrawals for groupmates who run the plugin.

## Privacy

Everything goes through RuneLite's own party service, and only to your group. If you want to be sure nobody can pretend to be one of you, everyone can set the same word in **Group Ironman secret**. The only other thing the plugin does is look up your own name on the hiscores when you log in, for your kill counts and ranks. Nothing goes anywhere else.
