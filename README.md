# The Group Ironman Homebase

Made for Group Ironman groups, so you can stop asking each other who has what. It puts the whole group in one sidebar panel: what everyone's carrying and banking, who took what out of group storage and how close you all are to your goals. It'll also settle who has the fastest Brutus. There's no website or account to sign up for, just the plugin.

## What's in it

- Everyone's stats, quests, diaries, combat achievements, collection log and boss kill counts, with PBs.
- Their inventory, gear, bank, seed vault and looting bag, and a search box for finding who has an item.
- A Storage tab with what's in group storage and who put in or took out what. Mark the things that should come back, and it shows who still owes them.
- Item requests and shared goals. You can put a bounty on a goal, which locks an item in group storage for whoever you pick until they reach it.
- A small HUD with everyone's HP, prayer, run energy and spec, and where they are. It knows the Sailing islands too.
- Your group on the world map, plus map pins, pings, shared tile markers and monster highlights.
- A catch-up when you log in, with the levels, quests, drops, pets, deaths and PBs you missed.
- A Compare tab that puts any two of you side by side, from levels to boss kill counts and PBs.
- Dots in your collection log, in each groupmate's colour, on the items they have. On boss pages, their kill counts and PBs sit next to yours.

## Getting started

Log in on your Group Ironman account, open the house icon in the sidebar and press **Connect**. The plugin finds your group by itself. Everyone in the group needs it, and you press Connect each time you start RuneLite.

Not a GIM group? One of you presses **Create group** and sends round the passphrase. The others paste it into the plugin's settings.

## Controls

- Middle-click the ground or a monster to ping it.
- Shift + right-click a monster for **Group highlight**, or a tile for **Group mark**.
- Right-click the world map for **Pin for group**.
- To find someone, click them in the HUD and then open your world map.
- Right-click an item on the Storage tab to say it needs returning.

## Good to know

- Data only moves while two of you are online at the same time. It gets passed along, though, so you still catch up on someone you're never online with.
- Banks and group storage update when someone opens them.
- PBs come from RuneLite's Chat Commands plugin, which is on by default.
- Bounties and items held for requests only stop withdrawals for groupmates who run the plugin.

## Privacy

Everything goes through RuneLite's own party service, and only to your group. Everyone in the party is checked against your group's member list. If anyone else is in it, nothing but your name is shared until they leave. You can stop sharing your position, items, drops or deaths in the settings. The only other thing the plugin does is look up your own name on the hiscores when you log in, for your kill counts. Nothing goes anywhere else.
